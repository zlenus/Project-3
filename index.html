Dataset chosen

I am using the Guttmacher Institute’s state-level abortion data, which covers all 50 states and the District of Columbia. Each column is a single measure from a single year, ranging from 2010 to 2020. Plan to focus on the measures that describe access to care and compare traveling across state lines. We want to ask whether limited local access pushes people to travel out of state for care.
